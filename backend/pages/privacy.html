Privacy Policy

R2R — personal finance transaction analysis

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy explains what R2R (“the app”) collects, why, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it. The app is published by {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}, who is the party responsible for the data described here. Questions go to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • R2R stores your bank statements, receipt photos and the transactions read from them, so it can answer questions about your spending.
  • To read and categorise them, it sends that content to an AI provider that you choose and connect with your own API key. This is the most important thing on this page.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time, immediately.

Things the app produces from them

Merchant names and short descriptions generated by the AI provider, extracted receipt line items, spending categories, and numerical “embeddings” that let the app search your data by meaning rather than by exact wording. These are derived from the data above and are deleted with it.

Things the app does not collect

  • No GPS coordinates. When you opt in to recording a shop, your device converts the location into a place name (for example “Main St, Norwalk”) and only that text is sent. No latitude or longitude ever reaches our servers or database.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. The app contains none. There is no advertising identifier, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no third-party marketing tags.
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone, or health data.
  • No bank credentials. R2R never asks for your online banking login and has no connection to your bank. It only reads files you export and upload yourself.

2. Who else sees your data

Your AI provider — read this part

R2R cannot read a receipt or categorise a transaction on its own. It sends that content to the AI provider you selected in Settings (currently Google or OpenAI), authenticated with the API key you supplied. What gets sent:

  • Photographs of your receipts and price tags, in full, as images;
  • transaction descriptions from your bank statements, to clean up merchant names and categorise them;
  • item descriptions from your receipts, to work out what a product is;
  • your chat messages, together with whatever transaction data the app retrieves in order to answer them.

Because you connect your own account with your own key, this traffic is governed by your agreement with that provider, not ours. Their policy determines whether they retain it, for how long, and whether they train on it. Please read it. Deleting your R2R account does not remove anything from your AI provider — to do that, contact them directly.

Web search, only if you turn it on

“Deep Enrichment” in Settings is off by default. Turning it on lets the app look up merchants and products on DuckDuckGo to describe them better. When it is on, merchant names and receipt item descriptions are sent to DuckDuckGo as search queries. Amounts, totals, dates and your identity are not. Leave it off and no search request is made.

Infrastructure providers

These process data on our behalf, under contract, and do not use it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage for your uploads.
  • Hugging Face — hosting for the application server.

Nobody else

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising or marketing, by us or by anyone. There are no data brokers involved. We disclose data otherwise only where the law requires it.

3. Location

Location is optional, off until you allow it, and used for one thing: naming the shop where you saw a price, because the same product costs different amounts in different shops and cities.

Before the app asks for the permission, it explains this on screen. If you allow it, your device takes a position fix and converts it to a place name on the device. Only that name is transmitted and stored. Declining costs you the shop name on a price note and nothing else. The app never requests background location.

4. Why we are allowed to process it

If you are in the UK, EU, or another region with similar law, our lawful bases are: performance of a contract for everything needed to provide the app you signed up for (storing your transactions, reading your receipts, answering your questions), and consent for the optional extras — location, and Deep Enrichment web search — each of which you can withdraw at any time by turning it off, without affecting anything else.

5. How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry, because the point of the app is comparing what you pay now against what you paid before.

You can delete your entire account from inside the app: Settings → Delete account. It is immediate and permanent, and it removes your login, every uploaded file, every receipt photo, all transactions, price notes and chat history, and your stored API key. There is no recovery period and no way for us to restore it afterwards. Full instructions, including what to do if you cannot sign in, are at /account-deletion.

You can also delete individual files or transactions at any time without deleting your account.

After deletion, server logs may briefly retain your account identifier as part of ordinary operational records. They contain no financial data, no file contents and no API keys, and they age out on our hosting providers’ normal schedules.

7. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Your AI provider API key is encrypted before it is written to the database, under a key held only in the server environment, so a database copy alone does not expose it.
  • Every row is scoped to its owner by database-level access rules, not only by application code.
  • Uploaded files are held in private storage and are reachable only through short-lived links issued to you.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will notify you and the relevant regulator as required by law.

8. Children
