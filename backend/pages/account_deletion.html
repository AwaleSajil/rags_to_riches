Delete your Rags2Riches data

Rags2Riches: Price Tracker (R2R) — published by {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}

From inside the app

  1. Open Rags2Riches (shown as R2R on your home screen) and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete my account, type your email address to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. There is no recovery step and no waiting period.

Delete some of your data, and keep your account

You do not have to delete your account to remove data from it. Inside the app you can delete, at any time and individually:

  • a transaction, from its detail screen;
  • an uploaded bank statement (CSV) or receipt photo, from the Files list;
  • a captured price tag;
  • a chat conversation, from the conversation drawer.

Each deletion is immediate and permanent, and takes with it anything derived from that item — deleting a receipt also removes the line items and price observations read from it.

What is deleted

Everything. Deleting your account removes:

  • your login and email address;
  • every bank statement (CSV) you uploaded, and every receipt or price-tag photo;
  • all transactions, line items, price observations, notes and chat history;
  • your saved AI provider API key.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing that identifies you is kept. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and it is not recoverable by us or by you.

Server logs may briefly contain your account identifier as part of ordinary operational records. These contain no financial data, no file contents and no API keys, and they age out on the hosting provider’s normal retention schedule.

Note that R2R sends transaction descriptions and receipt images to the AI provider whose API key you configured (for example Google or OpenAI) in order to read and categorise them. Anything that provider retains is governed by their policy and your account with them, and deleting your R2R account does not reach it. To remove that data, contact your AI provider directly.

If you cannot sign in

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address on the account, asking for deletion. We will confirm and delete it manually.

This page exists so account deletion can be requested without installing the app, as required by Google Play’s data deletion policy.
